Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

About the Application

Invoice Backup is a personal utility used to create and store backups of invoice and business database records.

Information and Data

The application processes database backup files belonging to its owner. These files are stored locally and may be uploaded to the owner's personal Google Drive account.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to obtain permission to upload backup files to Google Drive. Google account access is used only for the backup functionality.

Data Sharing

The application is intended for personal use. Backup information is not intended to be sold or shared with third parties.

Data Security

The application is designed to keep authentication credentials securely on the owner's computer.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the Google account used to configure the application.
